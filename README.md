## 🌍 Overview

**RouteMind** is an AI-powered travel planning application that helps users plan their complete journey from one place.

Instead of manually searching for transportation, hotels, tourist attractions, and activities across multiple platforms, RouteMind brings these tasks together into a single travel-planning workflow.

The user provides:

- Starting city
- Destination
- Travel date
- Number of days
- Total trip budget
- Hotel budget
- Travel interests

RouteMind then searches for relevant travel information and uses **Generative AI** to create a personalized travel itinerary.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🧭 Personalized Trip Planning

Enter your travel preferences and let RouteMind create a customized journey.

Users can specify:

- Starting location
- Destination
- Travel date
- Number of days
- Total budget
- Hotel budget
- Interests and activities

---

### ✈️ Transportation Comparison

RouteMind searches for available transportation options between the starting city and destination.

Supported travel modes include:

- ✈️ Flights
- 🚌 Buses
- 🚆 Trains

The application can compare available options based on information such as:

- Price
- Travel duration
- Departure
- Arrival
- Availability

This helps users identify suitable travel options according to their requirements.

---

### 🏨 Hotel Discovery

RouteMind searches for accommodation options based on the selected destination and hotel budget.

The hotel search workflow can provide information such as:

- Hotel name
- Price
- Location
- Available details
- Budget-friendly options

---

### 📍 Destination Discovery

Users can explore destinations and discover places to visit.

The application considers the user's interests while searching for attractions and activities.

Examples include:

- 🏖️ Beaches
- 🏔️ Mountains
- 🧗 Adventure
- 🏛️ Tourist attractions
- 🌆 Local exploration

---

### 🤖 AI-Powered Itinerary Generation

The main intelligence of RouteMind is its AI travel planner.

The system combines the collected travel information with user preferences and sends the relevant information to a Generative AI model.

The AI generates a structured **day-by-day itinerary**.

Example:

```text
Day 1
├── Arrival
├── Hotel Check-in
└── Local Exploration

Day 2
├── Beach Visit
├── Lunch
└── Sunset Activity

Day 3
├── Adventure Activity
├── Local Attractions
└── Evening Exploration

...

Day 5
├── Final Activities
└── Departure
